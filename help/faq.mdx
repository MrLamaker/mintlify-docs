## About Clamly

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Clamly?">
    A study app that keeps track of what you get wrong. You make quizzes and flashcards from your own notes, or let Clamly AI write them from a PDF, and Clamly brings the questions you missed back to you before you forget them. Notes, a focus timer, your exam dates and study groups live in the same place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who makes Clamly?">
    One student. I built Clamly because every study app I tried did too much, badly. It is run by Clamly SRL, a company registered in Romania. More on the [About page](https://clamly.app/about).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Clamly a homework answer service?">
    No. Clamly helps you learn your material. Clamly Chat will not write essays or assignments for you to hand in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which languages does Clamly speak?">
    The app itself is in English. Clamly AI works in your language: Clamly Chat answers in whatever language you write in, quizzes and flashcards are written in the language of your document, and note writing help can translate into 10 languages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Clamly free?">
    Yes, and the free plan is not a demo. Quizzes, flashcards, notes, todos, the timer, groups and exams are all included, with AI usage limited per session and per week. No card is needed to sign up. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do Starter and Pro add?">
    More AI: 4 times the weekly allowance on Starter and 8 times on Pro. Both read larger documents, give richer answers about your documents, and unlock advanced analytics. Pro also gets new features early through Labs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    **Plans**, then **Cancel subscription**. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. See [Cancel your subscription](/account/plans-and-billing#cancel-your-subscription).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a plan for my class or school?">
    Email [support@clamly.app](mailto:support@clamly.app) about a custom plan for a class or institution.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Studying

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can I upload to Clamly AI?">
    PDFs, Word documents (`.docx`) and plain text, up to 10 MB each. Clamly Chat also takes images. See [Clamly AI tools](/ai/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my AI usage limited?">
    Every AI request has a real cost to answer, so each plan has an allowance per session and per week. See [AI usage and limits](/ai/usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I study with friends?">
    Yes. Make a [study group](/together/groups), send people the invite code, and share quizzes and notes inside it. Nobody sees your scores unless you share them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I bring my cards from Quizlet or another app?">
    Yes. Export your Quizlet set and paste it into **Import** on the Flashcards or Quizzes page. Any other app works the same way if it can export one card per line, with a tab, a comma or ` - ` between the front and the back. See [Flashcards](/study/flashcards#make-a-deck).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Things that changed

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did coins, the shop and the leaderboard go?">
    They were removed in July 2026. Your streak and the 22 [achievements](/progress/streaks-and-achievements) remain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I still sign in with GitHub?">
    No. Sign in with your email and password, or with Google. If you used GitHub before, use [Forgot password?](/account/sign-in-and-security#reset-a-forgotten-password) with the same email address to get back in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is the How to Use page?">
    It moved here. These guides replace it, and they are linked from **Profile**, then **Help wiki**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do my flashcards come back at different times now?">
    Clamly switched from SM-2 to the FSRS scheduler on 21 September 2026. See [Why FSRS](/study/flashcards#why-fsrs).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
