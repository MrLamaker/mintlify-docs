Everything Clamly AI does uses **usage units**. You spend them from two allowances at the same time, and you can keep using AI while you have room in both.

## Your allowances

| Plan | Per session | Per week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | 28 units | 50 units |
| **Starter** | 50 units | 200 units |
| **Pro** | 120 units | 400 units |

**The session allowance** starts with your first AI request and refills **5 hours** later, whether or not you used all of it.

**The weekly allowance** starts with your first AI request of the week and resets **7 days** later.

<Note>
  A session is not the same as being signed in. Closing Clamly or signing out does not end it or refill it. Only the 5 hours do.
</Note>

## What each feature uses

| Feature | Units |
| --- | --- |
| Chat message | 1 to 3, by length, plus extras for attachments and quizzes. See [Clamly Chat](/ai/chat#what-it-costs). |
| AI writing help in a note | 1 |
| AI hint during a quiz | 1 |
| Highlight a document | 2 |
| AI review of a finished quiz | 3 |
| Study Planner, one week | 3 |
| Flashcard deck from a document or note | 4 |
| Quiz from a document | 5 |
| Study Pack | 1 to plan, then 4 per quiz, 3 for the deck and 2 for notes |

So on Free, one session covers about five generated quizzes, or a long evening of chat messages.

If a request fails partway, the units it reserved are given back automatically.

## See how much you have left

- **Starter and Pro:** the lightning meter in the header shows how much of your session allowance you have used, and says **Cooldown** while it refills. The **Billing** page shows both allowances, with a countdown to the next refill.
- **Free:** there is no meter. Clamly tells you when you reach a limit.

## When you reach a limit

You will see one of these messages. None of them affects anything that is not AI: quizzes, flashcards, notes and everything else keep working.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“You've reached your current session limit”">
    Your session allowance is used up. It refills 5 hours after the session started. Upgrading gives you a larger session allowance straight away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“You've used a lot of AI recently”">
    You hit the session limit and the session is cooling down. Wait until the 5 hours are up, or upgrade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“You've reached your weekly limit”">
    Your weekly allowance is used up. It resets 7 days after your first AI request of the week. Session refills do not add to it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Need more? Starter has 4 times the weekly allowance of Free, and Pro has 8 times. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).
</Tip>
