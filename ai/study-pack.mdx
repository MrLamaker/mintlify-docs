A Study Pack turns one document into everything you need to learn it: one or more quizzes, a flashcard deck that goes straight into spaced repetition, and optional study notes. Everything in the pack is linked to the same upload.

## Build a pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a document">
    Open **Clamly AI**, then **Study Pack**. Choose something you have already uploaded, or upload a new PDF, Word or text file up to 10 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the suggestion">
    Clamly reads the document and suggests a pack: how many quizzes, how many questions in each, and how many flashcards. The suggestion is sized to what the document can actually support, so a short handout gets a small pack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust it">
    Change the number of quizzes and questions, the difficulty and question types, the size of the deck, and whether to include **Study notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Choose **Create Study Pack**. When **Your Study Pack is ready** appears, your quizzes are in **Quizzes**, the deck is in **Flashcards**, and the notes are in **Notes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Quizzes per pack | 1 to 4 |
| Questions in the whole pack | Up to 50 |
| Flashcards | 4 to 40 |
| Document read | The same as for [quiz generation](/ai/overview#how-much-of-a-long-document-is-read) on your plan |

## What it costs

| Step | Units |
| --- | --- |
| Reading the document and suggesting a pack | 1 |
| Each quiz | 4 |
| The flashcard deck | 3 |
| Study notes | 2 |

One quiz and a deck comes to 7 units, less than making them separately. If a quiz or the notes fail to generate, the units for that part are given back.
