<Info>
  **Labs feature, Pro.** Study Planner is an early-access feature for Pro. When it is switched on for you, it appears on the **Early Access** page in the sidebar.
</Info>

The Study Planner looks at what is actually coming up for you and plans your week around it.

## What it reads

- Your upcoming **exams** and how close they are
- Your **classes**
- Flashcards that are **due**
- The quizzes you **score lowest** on

## Make a plan

1. Open **Early Access**, then **Study Planner**.
2. Choose **Generate my plan**. It takes a few seconds while Clamly plans your week.

<Tip>
  The plan is only as good as what Clamly knows. Add your exams on the [Exams](/study/exams) page and tag quizzes with a [class](/study/classes) first.
</Tip>

You get a week of up to 21 sessions, each between 10 and 120 minutes. Every session has a focus and an activity: a quiz, flashcards, notes, review, exam prep, or a break. Days with nothing planned show as **Rest day**.

## Use the plan

- Tick a session when you have done it. **Mark not done** undoes it.
- **Regenerate plan** writes a new week, for example after you add an exam.
- Delete a plan you no longer need.

Generating a plan uses **3 AI usage units**. If it fails, they are given back.
