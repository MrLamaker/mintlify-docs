Discover lists every quiz its owner has made **public**. Before you write a quiz from scratch, check whether someone has already made one.

## Find a quiz

- Switch between **Popular** and **Newest**.
- Use **Search public quizzes** to look for a topic.
- Open any quiz to play it, in any of the three [modes](/study/quizzes#play-a-quiz).

## Keep a quiz

Choose **Add to my quizzes** to save your own copy. It appears in **Quizzes** as yours, so you can edit it, add a class, or turn missed questions into flashcards.

## Share your own

Set any of your quizzes to **Public** and it appears on Discover. Set it back to **Private** or **Unlisted** to take it off. See [Share a quiz](/study/quizzes#share-a-quiz).

<Warning>
  A public quiz can be seen and copied by anyone. Leave out anything personal, and only share material you have the right to share.
</Warning>

## Report a quiz

If a public quiz is offensive, illegal, or copies your work without permission, report it with **Report** on the quiz, or email [support@clamly.app](mailto:support@clamly.app) with a link and the reason. Reports are reviewed and content is removed where appropriate.
