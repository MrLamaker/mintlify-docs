Deleting your account is permanent. There is no Trash for an account and no way to undo it.

## Before you delete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cancel your subscription">
    On Starter or Pro, cancel first on the **Plans** page. Deleting your account does not cancel your Stripe subscription for you. See [Cancel your subscription](/account/plans-and-billing#cancel-your-subscription).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export anything you want to keep">
    **Settings**, then **Export your data**, downloads a copy of your quizzes, notes, flashcards, chats and more. See [Export your data](/privacy/your-data#export-your-data).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over your study groups">
    Groups you own are deleted with your account, for every member. Let your group know first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete it

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Delete account**.
2. Type `delete my account` to confirm.
3. Choose **Delete Forever**.

## What is removed

Everything, in one step: your profile, quizzes and attempts, notes, todos, flashcards and their review history, exams, chat conversations, AI study plans, achievements, focus sessions, notifications, uploaded files and their search index, and your study group memberships. Groups you own are deleted too.

Your uploaded files and document search index are held by separate storage providers. Clamly deletes them straight away, retries automatically if a provider fails, and flags anything still outstanding so it is cleared within 30 days.

## What is kept

Invoices and payment records, for as long as accounting and tax law requires. That is longer than the account itself, and it is a legal obligation rather than a choice. Your card details are held by Stripe and never by Clamly.

<Note>
  Deleted your account and want to come back? You can sign up again with the same email, but you start from an empty account. Nothing from the old one can be recovered.
</Note>
