Pearl is Clamly's prize wheel at [pearl.clamly.app](https://pearl.clamly.app). Every Clamly account gets one free spin. You can also reach it from **More**, then **Pearl wheel**, on your phone.

## Spin

1. Open [pearl.clamly.app](https://pearl.clamly.app) and press spin.
2. Confirm the email address on your Clamly account.
3. Accept the official rules and continue.

Entry is free and needs no purchase.

## Who can enter

- Anyone with a Clamly account and a **verified** email address
- **One entry per account and per mailbox**, for as long as the promotion runs
- Entries must come from a personal address you normally use. Aliases, disposable addresses and addresses set up for extra entries are not eligible, and closing an account and opening a new one does not give you a second spin.

## The prizes

The pool includes 1, 3 or 6 free months of Starter, discounts of 20%, 30% or 50% off, a Clamly sticker pack, and Clamly merch.

Your prize is chosen at random **before** the wheel starts turning, and nothing during the spin changes it. Prizes are not equally likely: the slices are drawn the same size so the labels stay readable, not to show your chances. Quantities are limited and availability can change.

## Claim your prize

Your prize appears on screen and is **emailed to you**, so closing the page will not lose it. You have **90 days** from your spin to claim it.
