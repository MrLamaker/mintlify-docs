Deleting something in Clamly does not destroy it straight away. Quizzes, notes, todos and flashcard decks go to the **Trash** first, and you can restore them for **30 days**.

## Restore something

1. Open **Profile**, then **Trash**.
2. Find the item. Each one shows what it is: quiz, note, todo or flashcard deck.
3. Choose **Restore**. It goes back exactly where it was.

## Delete for good

- **Delete forever** on one item removes it permanently. It cannot be restored afterwards.
- **Empty trash** removes everything in the Trash, after you confirm.
- Anything left in the Trash is removed automatically **30 days** after you deleted it.

## What does not go to the Trash

| Item | What happens instead |
| --- | --- |
| A single flashcard | Deleted straight away, with a few seconds to undo from the message that appears |
| A chat conversation | Deleted permanently |
| A study group | Deleted permanently, by its owner |
| An uploaded document | Deleted straight away with its search index, **and so is the quiz generated from it**. Flashcard decks made from it stay. See [Delete an upload](/ai/overview#delete-an-upload). |
| Your account | Deleted permanently. See [Delete your account](/account/delete-account). |

<Tip>
  Something you deleted is not in the Trash? Check it was deleted less than 30 days ago, and that it is one of the four types above.
</Tip>
