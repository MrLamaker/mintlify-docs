The Exams page keeps every exam date in one place. Clamly uses it to remind you before each one and to tell the AI coach what is coming up.

## Add an exam

1. Open **Exams** and choose **Add New Exam**.
2. Enter the title and subject, then pick the date and time.
3. Add a description if it helps: topics, room number, what you are allowed to bring.

Exams that are close are flagged under **Needs attention**. When one is done, **Mark exam completed** so it drops off your upcoming list.

## Sync with Google Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    On the Exams page, connect Google Calendar and approve access in the Google window. Clamly asks to read and write calendar events, and uses that only for exam sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send exams to Google">
    Export all your upcoming exams at once, or use **Sync to Google Calendar** on one exam. Synced exams appear in your primary calendar with a 📚 in the title. If you change a synced exam, use **Update in Google Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring exams in from Google">
    Import looks at the next six months of your primary Google Calendar and brings in events whose titles contain words like *exam*, *test*, *quiz*, *midterm*, *final*, *assessment* or *review*. Events you already have in Clamly on the same day are skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Import only recognises those English words. If your calendar says *Klausur* or *examen*, add the exam in Clamly instead, or include one of the words in the event title.
</Tip>

### Disconnect Google Calendar

Disconnect from the Exams page at any time. Your exams stay in Clamly, sync stops, and Clamly deletes the access it was given.

## Reminders

Exam reminders arrive by email and, if you have turned them on, as push notifications. Switch them on or off under **Exam reminders** in [Settings](/account/settings#notifications).
