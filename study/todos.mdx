Todos is a task list built for studying. Keep it short and honest, and it becomes the one page you open every day.

## Add a todo

1. Open **Todos** and choose **New Todo**.
2. Write what needs to be done.
3. Pick a priority and, if it has one, a due date.
4. Choose **Create**.

| Priority | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Low** | Nice to do this week |
| **Medium** | Should happen soon |
| **High** | Needs doing before the next class |
| **Urgent** | Today, before anything else |

## Work through your list

- **Tick the circle** to mark a todo complete. Tick it again to reopen it.
- **Open a todo** to edit its title, priority and due date, and to add details, notes or steps. Changes save on their own as you type.
- **Pin** the todos that matter most so they stay at the top.
- **Drag and drop** to put the list in the order you will actually do it.
- **Select** puts the list in selection mode, so you can pick several todos, or all of them, and delete them together.
- **Search** your todos from the search box at the top of the page.
- **Add a class** to file a todo under a subject. See [Classes](/study/classes).

<Tip>
  Clamly Chat can help you plan your week around your todos. It only reads your todo titles when your message is about planning or scheduling. See [Clamly AI and your data](/privacy/ai-and-your-data#what-the-coach-knows-about-you).
</Tip>

## Delete a todo

Deleted todos go to the [Trash](/account/trash). You can restore them for 30 days.
