The Pomodoro timer alternates focused work with short breaks. Start it, then go and study: it keeps running while you move around Clamly.

## Start a session

1. Open **Pomodoro**.
2. Press start. The timer counts down your focus time, then switches to a break on its own, then back to focus.
3. The four marks under the timer show where you are in a set of four sessions.

The defaults are **25 minutes of focus** and a **5 minute break**. Open the timer durations to change them: focus can be 1 to 120 minutes, and a break 1 to 60.

## The mini timer

Once a session is running, a small timer stays pinned in the corner of every dashboard page. Go from a quiz to your notes to Clamly Chat and the countdown carries on.

<Note>
  If you reload the page or close the tab, a running timer is paused rather than lost. Open Clamly again and resume where you left off.
</Note>

## Your focus time

The Pomodoro page shows sessions and focus minutes for **this run**, **today** and **this week**. Your focus minutes also feed the study habits charts in [Analytics](/progress/analytics), and completed sessions count toward the Focused and Flow State [achievements](/progress/streaks-and-achievements).
