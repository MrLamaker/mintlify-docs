Clamly is free to start and needs no card. Every core feature is included on the Free plan, with AI usage limited per session.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [clamly.app/register](https://clamly.app/register).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm you are 16 or older">
    Tick the age box first. Both sign-up buttons stay locked until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sign in">
    Enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, or choose **Continue with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    With email and password, Clamly sends a verification link from `verify@mails.clamly.app`. Open it to activate your account. Google accounts are already verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You must be at least 16 to use Clamly. Clamly is run from Romania, where the age of digital consent is 16, and that threshold applies to everyone rather than changing by country.
</Note>

## Didn't get the verification email?

1. Check your spam or promotions folder for a message from `verify@mails.clamly.app`.
2. Try to [log in](https://clamly.app/login) with your email and password. Clamly answers **Email not verified** with a **Resend it** link. You can resend once every 90 seconds.
3. Still nothing after a few minutes? Email [support@clamly.app](mailto:support@clamly.app) from the address you signed up with.

## The five-step setup

After you verify, Clamly walks you through a short setup. It takes about two minutes, and you can't skip into the dashboard halfway through.

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Welcome** | A quick hello and what Clamly is for. |
| **About you** | Your display name, country and what you are studying for. The AI coach uses this to fit its advice to you. |
| **Mini quiz** | Three questions, so you see how a quiz feels. Nothing is saved. |
| **AI chat** | Your first message to Clamly Chat, with a real answer. |
| **Finale** | Your account is ready and you land on the dashboard. |

<Tip>
  If you first signed in with Google from the log in page instead of the sign-up page, setup also asks you to confirm your age. That is the only place it can be asked, since the log in page is for people who already have an account.
</Tip>
