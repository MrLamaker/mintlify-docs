Everything in Clamly lives under the dashboard at [clamly.app/dashboard](https://clamly.app/dashboard).

## The sidebar

On a computer, the sidebar on the left holds every section, in three groups.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Study">
    Quizzes, Discover, Notes, Todos, Flashcards, Study Groups, Classes, Pomodoro, Clamly AI and Exams.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Progress">
    Analytics and Achievements, plus Early Access when a Labs feature is open to you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Account">
    Plans, Profile, and Billing once you are on a paid plan. Settings is linked from Profile.
  </Card>
</Columns>

On a phone, the tab bar at the bottom has **Home**, **Quizzes**, **AI**, **Notes** and **More**. More opens everything else, grouped the same way.

## The header

The bar across the top of every dashboard page shows:

- **Your streak**, the number of days in a row you have studied.
- **AI usage**, on Starter and Pro: how much of your current session allowance you have used, or **Cooldown** while it refills. See [AI usage and limits](/ai/usage).
- **Search**, which opens the search palette.
- **Notifications**, the bell with reminders, achievements and announcements.
- **Light and dark mode**, the sun and moon toggle.

## Search everything

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux, or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac, from anywhere in the dashboard. Type part of a title and Clamly searches your quizzes, notes, todos, flashcard decks and study groups at once, showing up to five results for each.

<Tip>
  Search matches titles, not the text inside a note. Give notes a title you would search for later.
</Tip>

## The mini timer

Start a Pomodoro session and a small timer stays pinned in the corner while you move between pages. Going from a quiz to your notes does not cost you the session. See [Pomodoro](/study/pomodoro).

## Help from inside the app

- **Profile, then Help wiki** opens these guides.
- **More, then Wiki** does the same on a phone.
- The support chat bubble appears once you have allowed support cookies in the cookie banner.
