The **Analytics** page, under Progress in the sidebar, shows how your studying is going.

## The numbers at the top

| Figure | What it shows | Plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Avg score** | Your average quiz score | All |
| **Quizzes** | How many quizzes you have taken | All |
| **Focus time** | Hours of Pomodoro focus | All |
| **Day streak** | Your current [streak](/progress/streaks-and-achievements#your-streak) | All |
| **Peak score** | Your best day, and when it was | Starter and Pro |
| **Top class** | Your strongest subject and its average | Starter and Pro |

## The study report on Starter and Pro

Starter and Pro add a full study report over the last **30** or **90 days**. On Free, you see a blurred preview of it filled with sample data, never your own.

| Section | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Score trend** | Your average quiz score by day, with your lowest, average, peak and latest |
| **Accuracy by difficulty** | How you do on basic, intermediate and advanced questions |
| **Study habits** | Your daily average, your *sharpest day* for scores and your *deep-work day* for focus |
| **Focus time** | Pomodoro focus minutes for each day |
| **Performance by class** | Average score and attempts for each subject |

<Tip>
  **Performance by class** and **Top class** only count quizzes that have a [class](/study/classes). Tag your quizzes to see which subject needs the most work.
</Tip>

## Per-quiz analytics

Each quiz also has its own analytics on its page: how many times it has been played, the average score, and how often each question is answered correctly. See [Quizzes](/study/quizzes#after-a-quiz).
